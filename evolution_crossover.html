Conway Net — Crossover Evolution

The same genetic algorithm as the mutation-only Evolution page, but children can now be bred from two parents via crossover (recombination) instead of always cloning one parent and mutating it. Dial the crossover rate to 0% to reproduce the original mutation-only page exactly, or crank it up to see whether mixing two networks' weights helps or hurts.
